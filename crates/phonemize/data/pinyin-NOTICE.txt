The Chinese pinyin data in this directory — `pinyin-chars.txt`,
`pinyin-phrases.txt`, `pinyin-special.txt` and `pinyin-syllables.txt` — is
derived from two upstream projects. Neither is vendored: the files are
generated from them by `scripts/gen-pinyin-pro-data.mjs`, which reads them out
of `node_modules` and out of `pinyin-table.json` beside this notice.

pinyin-pro (https://github.com/zh-lx/pinyin-pro), MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2022-present zh-lx.

  `pinyin-chars.txt` is pinyin-pro's `DICT1` inverted: one line per character,
  its readings in pinyin-pro's priority order.
  `pinyin-phrases.txt` is `DICT2`–`DICT5` plus the numeral rule table built in
  `special.mjs`.
  `pinyin-special.txt` is `toneSandhiMap` and `toneSandhiIgnoreSuffix` from
  `special.mjs`, plus the two readings `handle.mjs` builds for 了 and 々.

misaki (https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki), MIT License.

  `pinyin-syllables.txt` is a transcription of `pinyin-table.json` in this
directory, which `scripts/gen-pinyin-table.py` generates from misaki's
`transcription.py` and pypinyin.
